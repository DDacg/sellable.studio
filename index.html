Questions

Good to know

How does payment work?+

Simple and low-risk: I build your ebook or sales page first, you review the finished result, and you only pay once you are happy with it. No money upfront.

Do I need to write or design anything?+

No. Bring the idea or topic and I handle the writing and design. If you already have your own content, I can format it and build the product around it instead.

What if I don't like the result?+

I will tweak it until it is right. And since you only pay after you see it, you are never stuck paying for something you do not love.

How long does it take?+

Most ebooks and sales pages are ready within a few days, depending on the length and scope of the project.

Do I need my own payment setup?+

For a sales page, yes: the checkout connects to your own Stripe or Gumroad, so you keep full control of your money. I walk you through setting it up, step by step.

How do we get started?+

Just reach out by email or text with your idea. I will come back with a plan and a quote, and we take it from there. Easy.
